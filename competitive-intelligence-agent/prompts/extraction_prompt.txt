Extract only facts supported by the supplied source document.

For each fact, return:
- company: The company name this fact relates to
- category: One of the requested analysis categories
- claim: A clear, factual statement
- evidence: A direct quote or passage from the source supporting the claim
- source_url: URL of the source
- source_title: Title of the source
- published_date: Publication date if available, otherwise null
- source_type: One of: official_website, official_documentation, regulatory_filing, official_blog, press_release, news, research_paper, github, job_posting, other
- confidence: Score from 0.0 to 1.0 based on how directly the source supports the claim
- is_company_claim: true if this is a marketing or promotional statement from the company

Rules:
- Do NOT add facts that are not present in the source
- Clearly mark opinions, marketing claims, and estimates
- Do NOT invent pricing numbers not stated in the source
- If pricing is not mentioned, do not create a pricing fact
- If information is ambiguous, lower the confidence score
- Separate facts from interpretations
